Every beat, from every app, in one crate.

Audio Crate is a Mac app for music producers. It gathers the beats, loops and voice notes people send you across iMessage, WhatsApp, Telegram, Signal, Discord, email and your own folders, and lets you listen, sort and send them from one place.

What it does

Finds your audio

Lists audio from your messaging apps, email and folders on your Mac, with who sent it and when.

Listens for you

Detects BPM, key and genre on your Mac, so you can filter by tempo, key or sound.

Review inbox

Play through what's new, mark files Hold or Follow up, and drop beats into crates.

Sends and schedules

Send beats to artists by iMessage or email, or schedule weekly packs.

Posts to YouTube

Turns a beat into a video with artwork, writes the title, description and tags, and uploads it to your own channel when you click Post.

Stays on your Mac

Audio Crate has no servers. Your files, messages and accounts never pass through us.

YouTube posting

If you choose to connect YouTube, Audio Crate asks Google for permission to upload videos to your channel and to read your channel's name, so it can show which channel a beat will be posted to. It uploads only the videos you choose to post, only when you click Post. You can disconnect at any time in Audio Crate's Settings, or remove access from your Google account permissions.
